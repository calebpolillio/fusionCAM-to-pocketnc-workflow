**Work and Tool Holding**

Here are some links and notes for selecting the right work holding and tools for your operation:


-mill plastic, wood, and soft metal pieces up to x4.55" × y5" × z3.55" (115.57 mm × 127 mm × 90.17 mm).

-move on five axes: the spindle travels 4.55" side to side; the table travels 5" front to back, 3.55" up and down, tilts 160°, and rotates 360°.

-Use ER11 collet for tool holding. There are a 8 different size options for the collet but the absolute minimum and maximum tool diameter is 0.109" (2.77mm) and 0.25" (6.35mm). Be sure your tool is within the grip range of the respective collet.
